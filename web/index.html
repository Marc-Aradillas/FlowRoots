About Us

Established in September 2024, Flow Roots is a Dallas-based organization created with the intention of uplifting the Dallas Popping community. Inspired by the legacy of John Doe, we aim to bring the same level of inspiration to dancers and enthusiasts alike.

Core Styles & Foundations

We preserve and cultivate street dance foundational knowledge through dedicated instruction:

Popping

Muscular contractions (pops/hits) timed to music beats, focusing on control, rhythm, and isolation.

Tutting

Geometric body angles and 90-degree wrist and arm positioning inspired by ancient Egyptian art forms.

Waving

Fluid, continuous energy transfer mimicking fluid dynamics and wave motion across body joints.

Animation & Gliding

Illusionist movement styles incorporating stop-motion mechanics, frame-rate control, and floating footwork.
