# Flowroots Platform Knowledge Base

## Overview
Flowroots is an urban popping dance crew established in September of 2024, arts platform, and educational initiative from the city of Dallas, Texas.

## Programs & Pricing
* Youth & Teen Drop-In Class: $20 / class
* Workshop Pass: $60 / 4-class pass
* Monthly Intensives: $125 / month
* Core Styles: Popping, Animation, Waving, Tutting, Memphis Jookin
